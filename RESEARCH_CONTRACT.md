# Research contract

1. **Truth and provenance.** Separate ideas, computational checks, independent review, and external evaluation. A test of tooling is not a proof. Preserve source IDs, timestamps, author attribution, methods, exact versions and hashes; distinguish claims from results. Never invent missing evidence or freeze a mutable worktree as an evaluated snapshot.
2. **Independent lanes.** Authors describe a falsifiable claim, inputs, assumptions, dependencies and reproducer. A different named researcher reviews the exact snapshot, searches for counterexamples, states limits, and signs a verdict. Self-review, including an author's other identity, is prohibited. Record conflicts and shared coordination; independence is an operational attestation, not something software can prove.
3. **Separate states.** Each entry has `research_status` (`idea`, `in_progress`, `candidate`, `reviewed`, `refuted`), `review_status` (`not_requested`, `pending`, `approved`, `changes_requested`), and `external_status` (`not_submitted`, `validating`, `accepted`, `rejected`, `unknown`). These are not interchangeable. Approved requires an independent approval on the exact snapshot; accepted externally is not a mathematical proof. `reviewed` requires approval. Status changes require evidence; a changed snapshot invalidates earlier reviews.
4. **Dependency and reproducibility.** Declare all known influences, including unverified ones, with locally registered IDs. No cycles or dangling IDs. Immutable snapshots require a regular-file manifest and verified hashes; no symlinks, traversal, or unmanifested files. Capture commands, environment, inputs, outputs and failure modes in the ledger; snapshots cannot depend on mutable external files without explicit disclosure.
5. **Safety and scope.** Work in the collaboration worktrees; do not modify the active challenge checkout. Push your own branch and evidence to the explicitly approved collaboration repository only; never force-push or overwrite another lane. Proposed solutions require another agent's independent review before integration. No autonomous Yukon submissions, PRs to upstream, external posts, or remote changes outside this repository without Leo's explicit approval. Read Yukon status without mutating it and record the observation time.
6. **Budget and experimental independence.** Treat 40/30/20/10 explore/review/integrate/final-check as a provisional schedule, not evidence of actual quota availability. Reserve verification capacity; if quota cannot be measured, mark it UNKNOWN and stop searching at the operator's direction. Freeze one initial attempt per lane before exchanging new results; record pre-existing exposure and do not claim a blind comparison if contamination occurred. Record shared versus independent baselines, resources, outcomes, failures and delayed review; no causal claim without a matched protocol.
7. **Evidence grades.** Keep conjectured, numerically reproduced, proof-checked, Yukon-evaluated and independently mathematically reviewed distinct. A non-author must review the exact snapshot, disclose dependencies, attempt falsification and record verdict and limitations; no self-promotion to independently verified. A machine verdict is not human proof review. Never silently overwrite a prior immutable snapshot.
